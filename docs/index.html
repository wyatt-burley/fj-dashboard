Quick views
Velocity = trailing-30-day units (all channels, FBA + FBM) ÷ 30 = units/day.
FBA Days = FBA sellable stock ÷ velocity → the tier: OUT 0 in FBA   HIGH under 7 days   LOW 7–14 days   OK 14+ days.
Total Days adds FBM stock on top of FBA (the backstop if FBA runs dry — slower shipping, but the sale isn't lost).
Sales Risk: AT RISK = FBA + FBM and inbound together still cover under 14 days — these dollars are genuinely exposed; restock decisions start here. BUFFERED = FBA is short but FBM stock or inbound already on its way restores cover — watch, don't panic.
Why four sales windows? 1-day catches what moved yesterday; 7-day smooths out one big order distorting the rate; 30-day sets the restock math; 12-month shows seasonality so winter sends aren't sized off August sales. Check the 12-mo trend before trusting any single window.
